Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes — every assessment, score and health tip is free. Premium is an optional add-on for AI-generated action plans.

How accurate are the assessments?

They use internationally validated tools such as PHQ-9, GAD-7 and IDRS with standard scoring. They are screening aids, not a diagnosis.

Is my data private?

Yes. Results stay on your device unless you save them to your account, and we never sell or share health information.
